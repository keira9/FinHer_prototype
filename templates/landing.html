Creditworthiness, redefined for
women entrepreneurs in Africa

More than 58 million women run micro and small businesses across Africa — yet most remain invisible to formal credit systems built around collateral, payslips, and bank histories they were never meant to have.

I'm a Woman Entrepreneur → I'm a Lender / Admin →

The Problem

Traditional credit scoring excludes women in the informal sector — even though 70% of East Africa uses mobile money, and SACCOs serve tens of millions of women savers. This data exists. It's just never been used. The result: a $42 billion financing gap for women-owned businesses across Africa.

Our Solution

FinHer reads the financial life women entrepreneurs actually live — and turns it into a score lenders can trust.

💳

🤝

🛒

Informal Trade Activity

Market trading volume and frequency reflect a business that's actually running.
